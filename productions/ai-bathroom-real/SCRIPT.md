# Script — AI Bathroom, Made Real

English voiceover, ~97 s, 9:16. Beats are mapped to the reference
(`references/bikin-kamar-mandi-ai/TIMELINE.md`); wording and props are new.
`OVR:` is the loud overlay label for that beat. `VIS:` is the still-image subject;
`MOT:` is the animation direction for the video model.

Persona: 24-year-old American woman, auburn wavy hair, freckles, light blue denim shirt.
Persona anchor: `assets/persona/persona-key-v1.png`.
