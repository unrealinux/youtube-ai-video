# Timeline: "Bikin Kamar Mandi AI Jadi Nyata"

Times are source-media seconds. Evidence: `evidence/overview-6x6.jpg` (36 labeled frames).

## 0.0–15.0 · Hook and target

Creator in a blue shirt, selfie/medium, red hair, talking to camera. Yellow overlay "BIKIN KAMAR
MANDI AI" top-left. Quick inserts of the AI bathroom render: an overhead white basin with a red
rim, water running over a white sink. She points and reacts. At ~12.2 s a title card "ayo bongkar"
(let's tear it down). At ~14.9 s she is beside the real, dirty toilet with a hand-written note
"this is what the AI made". Function: state the premise and show the target in under 15 s.

## 15.0–26.0 · The real room is bad

Overlay/subtitle "lantai kek jamuran…" (the floor is mouldy). Hands point at the floor and ceiling.
Insert of a tap with a white bottle. At ~23 s she stands next to a small white basin, laughing.
Function: establish the gap between the AI render and reality.

## 25.7–40.0 · Building the fake washbasin

Overlay "MULAI DARI WESTAFEL" (start from the washbasin, ~25.7 s). She sits on the floor cutting a
cardboard "AI" target (~28.4 s). Then she holds a green umbrella over a red basin (~31.1 s),
mixing/pouring inside the basin (~33.8 s), placing a concrete block with an arrow overlay labeled
"pemberat" (weight) inside the basin (~36.5 s). This is the physical build of the AI basin prop.
Function: the first absurd construction; the "weight" label is a comic detail.

## 40.0–50.0 · Comparison beat

Overlay "REAL or FAKE" (~41.8 s), split-looking beat while she works over a red basin. Then
"aku ikut contoh" / "aku ikut contoh" (I follow the example) near a large round basin (~44.6 s).
Two people handle the basin at ~47.4 s. Function: mark the manual labour and the copying-of-the-render
beat.

## 50.0–60.0 · Reveal of the fake washbasin

Overlay "JADI WESTAFEL AI" (becomes the AI washbasin, ~50.0 s). She stands proudly beside a red
pedestal basin with a red cloth skirt. Then a close-up of a hand at the fabricated basin (~52.8 s),
she talks to camera at ~55.4 s, then an undershot of the aquarium/fish tank (~58.2 s) with her arms
raised.

## 60.0–72.0 · Aquarium and flush subplot

Overlay "aku udah ambil" (~60.9 s) while she cleans a white toilet. She sits beside an orange plastic
bag and a white toilet (~63.6 s). She carries the aquarium (~66.3 s), overlay "aku beli ikan robot"
(I bought robot fish, ~68.9 s) over scattered aquarium decorations. The aquarium is assembled at
~71.7 s.

## 72.0–82.0 · Installation

Overlay "pasong flush" (install the flush, ~77.1 s) with a hand lowering a flush mechanism. She
stands beside the toilet and the aquarium (~74.4 s). Function: complete the improvised installation.

## 82.0–97.4 · Result and open ending

Overlay "HASILNYA 😘" (the result) at ~82.5 s: the finished toilet plus aquarium. Undershot of water
running into the basin at ~85.2 s. Then the turn: she stands looking at the phone (~87.9 s),
subtitle "gimana ini 😅" (how is this) and "ternyata susah…" (turns out it's hard, ~90.6 s). Final
two beats at ~93.3 and ~96.0 s: "SARAN GES GIMANA INI???" (suggestions, guys, how?!) over the
unfinished toilet/basin. Function: withhold the satisfying resolution and hand the problem to the
audience.
