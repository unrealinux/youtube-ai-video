# Reference: "Bikin Kamar Mandi AI Jadi Nyata" — Irene Suwandi

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jtWykNJMdWY
Local: `source.mp4` (608×1080, 30 fps, 97.4 s, Indonesian speech, vertical 9:16)
Format: creator-led vertical social short (TikTok / Reels / Shorts), handheld selfie A-roll + process B-roll.

## Whole-piece reading

One female creator sets out to recreate an AI-generated bathroom design in real life. The promise is
in the title ("Making the AI bathroom real"). The humour is that the AI design is impractical: the
build gets consumed by a fake concrete washbasin, an umbrella-held basin, a goldfish aquarium next to
the toilet, and a flush that does not work. The piece ends on an unresolved complaint and a direct
call for help from the audience ("suggestions, guys — how?!"), which is the engagement hook.

The reference works because it is a **challenge/reveal format with a counter-intuitive payoff**: the
viewer expects a satisfying makeover and instead gets a relatable failure. Progress is shown as a
sequence of small, visibly funny attempts. The creator's reactions (surprise, frustration) carry the
entertainment; the words explain the joke.

## Recurring systems

1. **Creator A-roll (selfie, eye contact, talking to camera).** Establishes persona and narrates.
   Present at the hook, after most steps, and at the ending. She is the emotional anchor.
2. **Process B-roll / inserts (hands, objects, fixtures, mock-ups).** Close shots of building,
   mixing concrete, holding an umbrella over a basin, transferring fish, installing a flush.
3. **AI-render inserts.** Flattened "AI bathroom" images used as the reference target and as a
   comparison ("REAL or FAKE"). These establish what she is trying to reproduce.
4. **Big overlay text (CapCut-style).** Yellow/white bold Indonesian labels with colored shadow,
   top-left or center: "BIKIN KAMAR MANDI AI", "MULAI DARI WESTAFEL", "REAL or FAKE",
   "JADI WESTAFEL AI", "HASILNYA 😘", "SARAN GES GIMANA INI???". These title each beat and hold the
   viewer's place. Some are static labels, one is a two-state comparison.
5. **Ambient/location sound + speech.** No music bed evident from the overview; see transcript and
   audio check for confirmation.

## Why it holds attention

- Hook states the goal and the visual target in the first 3 s.
- Each beat is a new absurd attempt, roughly 2–4 s apart; there is no dead air.
- The "REAL or FAKE" comparison and the "pemberat" (weight) detail reward close watching.
- The ending withholds resolution and asks the audience, which drives comments.
